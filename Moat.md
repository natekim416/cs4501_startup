We believe that our moat is our LLM's validation mechanisms and our incoming benchmark. Cleanasauras asks users to approve the clean-up 
plan before it is put into action, so users can validate the LLM. Our prompting features also allow you to delete specific items that many current cleanup tools wouldn't catch. To test this we will be implementing a benchmark that compares our product with other companies based on what files we want deleted and not.
We want to show current and potential users quantitative (and qualitative) numbers that show that our product is better than others.
